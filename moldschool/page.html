A note from my studio

Hi, I'm Michelle

I make every one of my molds by hand in my little studio in Port Alberni, on Vancouver Island. Over the years I've answered the same lovely questions again and again. How much glass do I need? Zyp or kiln wash? Why did my piece stick? So I gathered all of it into one place for you.

Mold School is what I wish I'd had when I started. No jargon and no pressure, just the tips that really make a difference, shown at my kiln, step by step. Fused glass is easier than you think, and I'd love to help you get beautiful results from your very first firing.

XO, Michelle

Your questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. Mold School is my gift to everyone who makes with my molds. No credit card and nothing to buy.

I haven't bought a mold yet. Can I still join?

Absolutely. Come and have a look around. You'll see exactly how the molds work before you choose one.

How do I log in?

Once you sign up, I'll email you a login link. You can also log in any time at community.michellefrost.com.

Where do I find my instruction sheet?

Inside Mold School, open the Mold Instructions module. There's a lesson for each mold with a printable sheet you can keep by your kiln.

I'm stuck. Can I ask you?

Of course. Email hello@michellefrost.com with a photo of your piece and I'll help you work out what happened.
