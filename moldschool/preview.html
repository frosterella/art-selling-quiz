Hi, I'm Michelle

I make every one of my molds by hand in my studio in Port Alberni, on Vancouver Island. Makers ask me the same great questions again and again. How much glass do I need? How do I prep my mold? Why did my piece stick? So I gathered all of the answers into one place for you.

Mold School is brand new, and it's what I wish I'd had when I started. No jargon and no pressure, just the tips that really make a difference, shown at my kiln, step by step. Fused glass is easier than you think, and I'd love to help you get beautiful results from your very first firing.

Your questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. Mold School is free for everyone who makes with my molds. No credit card and nothing to buy.

I haven't bought a mold yet. Can I still join?

Absolutely. Come and have a look around. You'll see exactly how the molds work before you choose one.

How do I log in?

Once you sign up, I'll email you a login link. You can also log in any time at community.michellefrost.com.

Where do I find my instruction sheet?

Inside Mold School, open the Mold Instructions module. There's a lesson for each mold with a printable sheet you can keep by your kiln.

Something went wrong with my piece. Where do I start?

Head to the Troubleshooting module. It walks through bubbles, thin spots, sticking and more, with what causes each one and how to fix it next time.
