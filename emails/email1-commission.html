Why it suits you

Commissions are sold before you make them. You're paid (usually with a deposit up front) and the buyer gets something personal. Interior designers, art consultants and home stagers are always looking for artists who can work to a brief, size and colour palette. One good designer relationship can bring you repeat projects for years, and every happy private client tells their friends.

Watch out for

Without clear boundaries, commissions can eat your time and your creative energy. Offer a set menu of sizes and prices, take a non-refundable deposit, limit revisions, and put it all in writing. Designers usually expect a trade discount (often 10–20%), so build it into your prices from the start.

Your first three steps

1Decide what you'll offer (subjects, sizes, turnaround) and set your prices.
2Make two or three sample pieces for friends or family to use as examples.
3Create a simple commission page with your prices and examples, then introduce yourself to two or three local interior designers or home stagers.

The Custom Collaborator's Getting-Started Checklist

Decide what you'll offer: subjects, sizes and materials, up to statement pieces
Know your Minimum Price and Ideal Price, and price the whole job: design, revisions, framing and delivery
Set starting prices for each option so you're not quoting from scratch
Make two or three sample pieces to show what you do
Write out your process: enquiry, deposit, sketch, final piece, delivery
Decide your deposit (often 50%, non-refundable) and how many revisions you include
Create a simple agreement covering price, timeline, revisions, deposit and copyright
Make a commission page or enquiry form with examples and starting prices
Set your trade discount for interior designers and art consultants (often 10–20%)
Introduce yourself to two or three local designers, and to businesses that buy corporate gifts
Check your business licence, tax registration and insurance for work in progress
Photograph every finished commission, and ask happy clients for a testimonial and a referral
